# Questions for Lisa

Business decisions the booking and accounts work needs. Until answered, the app takes the default shown.

1. **Invoicing before the supplier invoice:** may a Client be invoiced in Quicktrav before the XL or supplier invoice arrives? If so, under what conditions? *Default: allowed; the Item shows as invoiced with its supplier invoice still awaited.*
2. **Editing after verification:** who may change an Item's cost, Markup or fees once an Accountant has verified its supplier invoice? Is an approval needed? *Default: only Administrators.*
3. **Online saving:** what counts as a significant saving, and must the online fare have the same baggage and fare rules? *Default: no flag until a threshold is set.*
4. **Payment accounts:** the exact card and account nicknames to use (Company Services card, Company card, TTD card?), and which statement formats (CSV, PDF, bank) you could export for reconciliation later.
5. **Cash vs EFT vs card:** in which workflows does each apply, and who is the payer and recipient in each? When a Client's own card (e.g. the Company Services card) pays a Supplier directly, does the Client still pay the full Quicktrav invoice, or only the agency's fees? *Default: the card Payment counts only as paid to the Supplier; the Client invoice is settled by the Client's own Payments.*
6. **Reminders:** which reminders may go out by themselves, which need approval, and who approves them? (Planned for phase 2.)
7. **Retention:** how long must Trips, invoices and payments be kept? The brief asks for at least four years; your accountant should confirm South African tax and POPIA obligations.
8. **XL invoice lag:** does XL invoice after a predictable number of days? If so, the expected supplier-invoice date could default to it. *Default: the Consultant types it.*
9. **Access:** every User, including an external bookkeeper with an Accountant account, will see every Client's contact details, Trips and prices. Only ID and passport copies stay with the Client's Consultant and Administrators. Is that acceptable?
10. **Quicktrav:** one Quicktrav invoice number per charge, or several invoices and credit notes per service? Could Quicktrav export or import data, so numbers need not be typed?
11. **Refunding before the supplier does:** may a Client be paid back before the supplier's refund arrives? *Default: allowed; the Refund is flagged as out of pocket until the supplier pays.*
12. **Crediting a refund:** when an issued Item is cancelled and the supplier refunds, is the Client credited the refund less our fee on one Quicktrav credit note for the cancellation? And when an Item is cancelled before it is issued, is everything invoiced for it credited back and our fee invoiced separately? *Default: yes to both; if the supplier refunds less than our fee, the rest of the fee is invoiced.*
